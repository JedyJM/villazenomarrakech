The villa

Quiet in the Palmeraie, with the Atlas on the horizon

The drive to Villa Zen'O takes you through Marrakech’s Palmeraie. Beyond the entrance, a cactus garden opens onto an infinity pool, with the Atlas rising behind it.

The architecture brings together contemporary lines and Moroccan tradition: an open living space, high ceilings and wide glass doors give the house an indoor-outdoor feel. Each suite has its own bathroom, with a calm outlook towards the pool or the olive trees.

The house works well for families and groups of friends. It is all on one level, with a cot, a high chair, games, and a discreet team on hand.

Villa Zen’O is a nod to Dragon Ball Z: a statue of Son Goku watches over the garden.

FAQ

Questions you may have

How many guests can the villa accommodate?

Villa Zen'O can accommodate up to 8 guests across 4 suites and 5 beds, with 4 bathrooms and 1 separate toilet.

Can the swimming pool and jacuzzi be heated?

Yes, both can be heated on request:

  • Swimming pool: from €60 per day.
  • Jacuzzi: from €35 per day.

Rates vary depending on the season. Please request heating at least 48 hours in advance to ensure everything is ready for your arrival.

What services are included in the stay?

Your stay includes house staff on site from 9 am to 5 pm, 24-hour security, pool cleaning, garden maintenance, fibre Wi-Fi up to 200 Mbps, bed linen, bath and pool towels, and complimentary bathroom amenities (soap and shampoo). Kitchen facilities and leisure equipment, including a basketball hoop, are also available.

Your stay

Can we invite visitors to the villa?

Villa Zen'O is exclusively reserved for the guests listed on the booking. For security and privacy reasons, outside visitors are not permitted.

Is the villa suitable for families with children?

Yes. The villa is all on one level and offers a baby cot, high chair, children's tableware, books and toys, as well as a PS5, table football, table tennis and a basketball hoop. Children must always be supervised by their parents around the swimming pool and jacuzzi.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out is before 11 am. Any requests for alternative timings are subject to availability and must be confirmed in advance.

Services and dining

Is daily housekeeping provided?

Our house staff are at the villa from 9 am to 5 pm. Housekeeping is arranged according to your preferences, and daily cleaning can be provided on request.

How does the dining service with Mama Fouzia work?

Mama Fouzia prepares your meals at the villa, according to the following schedule and rates:

  • Breakfast (9 am–12 pm): €10 per person.
  • Lunch (12 pm–4 pm): €15 per person.
  • Dinner (7 pm–9 pm): €20 per person.

Meals should be requested the day before. Alternatively, you can purchase your own groceries, and Mama Fouzia will prepare your meals at no additional service charge. Groceries remain at your expense.

What is on Mama Fouzia's menu? Can we request other dishes?

The menu features a selection of Moroccan specialities and family favourites. It can be fully tailored to your preferences. You are welcome to share your favourite dishes, dietary requirements or special requests before or during your stay.

Do you offer a delivery service for groceries or other requests?

Yes. Our security attendant can take care of small grocery runs and occasional requests during your stay, for added comfort and convenience. The fee is 100 MAD (€10) per delivery.

Getting around Marrakech

Do we need to hire a car?

We recommend renting a car to explore Marrakech and its surroundings at your own pace, although it is not essential. Uber is available, and we can also arrange private drivers for your journeys.

How much is a trip from the villa to the medina?

Uber rides to the Medina start at 100 MAD (10 €). Private drivers can also be arranged from 250 MAD (25 €) per journey, depending on the number of passengers.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes, we can arrange transfers to and from Marrakech Airport. Prices start at 350 MAD (35 €) per transfer. Simply share your flight details and the number of passengers in advance so we can arrange your welcome.

What activities can be arranged from the villa?

From the villa, we can arrange quad biking, camel rides, horse riding and relaxing massages at the villa. Please book these activities in advance through our team.
